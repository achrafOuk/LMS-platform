# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Learners and career-focused professionals who want structured online courses with clear progress tracking. Secondary audience: instructors and organizations evaluating an LMS platform for team training.

## Product Purpose

An LMS platform that helps people learn new skills, complete courses, and earn credentials through a single intuitive experience. Success means learners can discover courses, enroll, track progress, and complete certifications without friction.

## Brand Personality

Clear, confident, approachable. The voice is expert but welcoming—not corporate or cold. Visitors should feel the platform is trustworthy and built for real learning outcomes.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS hero templates (avatar stacks, star ratings, fake partner logos)
- CSS placeholder dashboards instead of real product or learning imagery
- Over-decorated card grids and ghost-card shadows
- Timid beige landing pages with no visual commitment

## Design Principles

1. **Show the product** — Use real screenshots or credible learning imagery; never fake UI mockups as hero visuals.
2. **One proof point beats five metrics** — Prefer a specific testimonial or fact over stacked social-proof badges.
3. **Clarity over decoration** — Typography and spacing carry hierarchy; avoid scaffold eyebrows and dividers on every section.
4. **Accessible by default** — Semantic HTML, visible focus, skip links, and decorative visuals hidden from assistive tech.
5. **Committed color** — Primary blue and gold secondary carry brand identity; neutrals support, not dominate.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus states, reduced motion preferences, and sufficient text contrast (4.5:1 body, 3:1 large text). Decorative hero imagery must not pollute the document outline.
